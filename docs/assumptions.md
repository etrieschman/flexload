Update in the same commit as the code that changes it.

| # | assumption | where | status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Investment costs** by RTS-GMLC category are placeholders ($/kW-yr: nuclear 600, coal 350, CC 110, CT 80, oil ST 300, hydro 300, wind 140, PV 90, CSP 500), annual on the scenario, prorated by T/8760 where used. RTS-GMLC has no capital costs. | `system.INVESTMENT_COST_PER_KW_YEAR` | placeholder; replace with a cited table before any result depends on the investment layer |
| 2 | **Cost of the investment layer** Cⁱⁿᵛ is annual: max(0, Σⱼ (cⁱⁿᵛⱼ − Σ_{t∈year} νⱼₜ) ūⱼ) on the B0 path over `windows(...)`; the window's share is Cⁱⁿᵛ·T/8760, the same in every window. The floor at zero (Erich, 2026-10-21): a market whose prices overpay the fleet has nothing left to collect, so the layer bills zero on the peak hour instead of rebating rents to the load. Not called missing money: that is what a price cap leaves, and there is no cap. Cached in `~/.cache/flexload/C_inv` by market fields and the year's data. Rejected: the week's own missing money (a peak week's rents exceed its cost share and the pool goes negative, −$178M on week 31) and allocating to scarcity weeks (keep in reserve if an RQ needs it). | `Market.C_inv`, `system.C_inv` | decided 2026-10-19 (Erich) |
| 3 | **ORDC** is fit to the data: π(z) = VOLL·Pr[ε > z − R̲] as ten steps spaced evenly in price, widths from the normal quantiles. ε is the hourly net-load forecast error (real-time less day-ahead load, wind and solar), fit as N(0, σ²) with one σ for the year from RTS-GMLC's two series; R̲ is the largest unit's output, the contingency PJM's row protects against. ERCOT fits ε by season and hour block and sets R̲ at 3,000 MW; both are refinements. Under ERCOT, C_sys includes −Σₜ Π_t, large and negative when reserve is free. | `Scenario.epsilon_std`, `ORDC.from_lolp`, `Market.ordc_for` | decided 2026-10-21 (Erich: one σ for the year); placeholder curve removed |
| 4 | **ERCOT formulation**: no per-unit contingency row; load-following γ d_sys is hard and only reserve above it is valued by the curve, Π_t = Σ_s π_s z_st. PJM: one row per unit, largest running unit plus γ d_sys, hard; Π_t = 0. `C_sys` includes −Π_t in both. | `Market.constraints`, `Market.C_sys` | as the report |
| 5 | **Reserve offer cost** cʳ = 0. RTS-GMLC has none; the knob was removed 2026-10-19. | `scenario` | default |

| 7 | **Outages**: none. The Markov-chain option was removed 2026-10-19 (git 171cf6e has it); restore when an RQ replicates over outage seeds. | — | removed |
| 8 | **Carve-out**: δ = α × system load, hour by hour; d° = total − δ. System load at B0 equals the data. Area profiles removed 2026-10-19. | `scenario` | decided 2026-10-13 |
| 9 | **Tightness**: `reserve_margin` scales both loads so the *tightest hour* has that margin. In `windows(...)` one scale factor serves the year, so the annual tightest hour has the margin and other windows keep their seasonal slack; `scenario(reserve_margin=)` on one window scales that window alone. The default 0.15 is calibrated so ERCOT's energy-only prices pay the fleet's annual investment cost (rents 2,516 M$ against 2,500 M$; PJM's rents 1,443 M$, missing money 1,057 M$). Above 25% reserve is never scarce and the two markets earn the same rents. | `tighten`, `windows` | decided; annual scaling 2026-10-19; margin 0.15 2026-10-21 (Erich) |
| 9b | **Other load sheds at VOLL** when capacity is short (`Dispatch.shed`), a real cost in C_sys; λ and μ reach VOLL in those hours. | `Market.clear` | decided 2026-10-13 |
| 10 | **Own share.** The exogenous price-taker takes the posted average rate Cᵏ/(x̂ + x°) at its believed consumption x̂ = δ, as ERCOT posts a provisional load-ratio share. The consistent price-taker knows its charge is its share of the pool and pays along the tangent of Cᵏ x/(x + x°) at x̂, slope Cᵏ x°/(x̂ + x°)², with x̂ the last iterate of the fixed point; iterating makes it exact (the convex-concave procedure). Either way the bill is linear in d, so the best response is a QP, and exact at settlement where x̂ = x. | `Share.charge(knows_share)`, `best_response(knows_share)`, `fixed_point` | revised 2026-10-21 (Erich): own share is part of the belief |
| 11 | **Two-part lump sum** T = (share of expected base) × (pool − φ × expected total base), with the expected base at `d_hat`. φʳᵉˢ = γμₜ, φⁱⁿᵛ = 0 as the memo sets them. `T` may be given explicitly. | `TwoPart.lump_sum` | decided; apportionment rule is the memo's open todo |
| 12 | **Everyone else's bill** Φ∘ is the same tariff with d and d° swapped, no credit, no exemption. | `settle(own=False)` | default |
| 13 | **Offer where it will not clear**: the load offers σ = 0 in hours where μ̄ < cˢ, as the memo states, rather than leaving the QP indifferent. | `best_response` | as the memo |
| 14 | **Curtailment anticipation** follows the belief (report Table 2): the exogenous price-taker consumes its bid; the consistent price-taker expects dᶠⁱʳᵐ in the hours where its believed energy price is at VOLL. κ is gone. | `payoff(anticipate, voll)`, `fixed_point` | revised 2026-10-21 (Erich) |
| 15 | **Solvers**: Gurobi for the LP and the QPs (academic license; 3× HiGHS on the clearing, identical duals on week 31). Duals at degenerate merit-order points are solver-dependent. | `Market.solver`, `best_response(solver=)`, `planner(solver=)` | changed 2026-10-19 |
| 16 | **Named types** θ₀…θ₄ run as a business: data center (ψ high, β medium, A = 1ᵀ) or bitcoin mine (ψ low, β high, A = 0); emergency fixed at (ψ medium, β low, A = 0, p high); controllable p low. | 17 | **Named tariffs**: τ₀ only, every slot explicit; the investment hours 𝒲ⁱⁿᵛ are the ex-ante peak hour t* from the B0 path. Workshop II adds the rest. | `named_tariffs` | Erich, 2026-10-21 |
| 19 | **Planner for a curtailable type** holds the headroom instrument, d − (1−β)δ toward reserve in every hour, but consumes what it bids: letting it consume below the bid let it dodge energy conservation (S* exceeded any realized S by 5% on the test scenario). Curtailment below the bid is the operator's scarcity action only. | `planner` | revised 2026-10-19 |
| 20 | **Curtailable firm level** is a MW level constant over the window, dᶠⁱʳᵐ = (1−β) minₜ δₜ, as ERCOT SB6 and PJM Non-Firm Contract Demand use a level; the load declares it before the window. It is the largest cut the type tolerates in its lowest hour, so a curtailment elsewhere can exceed the tolerance band and V prices it. The LP curtails only where κ > VOLL. | `LoadType.d_firm` | decided 2026-10-21 (Erich); was a profile |
| 21 | **Operator's rule** is max-min welfare over Θ without the inflexible load, or expected welfare under a prior, with g(ρ) ≤ 0 per layer or on the total; regret is not used. | `choose` | decided 2026-10-21 (Erich) |
| 22 | **Price error** of a belief is mean_t \|λ̃_t − λ_t\| between the prices the bid answered to and the prices it produced; for the consistent price-taker that is the last iterate's gap. | `summary`, `fixed_point` | 2026-10-21 |
| 18 | **Long run**: y ≥ 0 per offer block against the block's availability shape; no minimum build, no retirement cost. | `Market.constraints` | default |
